## 1. Executive Summary

**The Opportunity**

The global restaurant technology market is accelerating hard into AI. According to Toast's 2025 Voice of the Restaurant Industry Survey, 86% of restaurant operators are already comfortable using AI and 81% plan to expand their usage. Yet most independent and mid-size restaurants are underserved - the AI tools that exist are either too expensive, too complex, or too generic to deploy without a dedicated tech team.

**The JV**

Henry Mascot (AI/agentic systems expertise) and Unik Ernest (hospitality industry knowledge, relationships, and operational credibility) form a joint venture to build and sell AI agent solutions specifically designed for restaurants and hospitality businesses.

**What We Are Selling**

AI agents that act as digital staff members - handling the repetitive, time-consuming operational tasks that restaurant managers and owners currently do manually or ignore entirely because they have no time.

Think of it as giving a restaurant a back-office AI team that never sleeps, never forgets, and never costs overtime.

**What Makes This Different**

Most restaurant tech sells software licenses. We sell outcomes. We configure, deploy, and manage AI agents that deliver measurable results - reduced labor waste, fewer missed calls, better inventory turns, and higher review scores.

**Market Timing**

- 87% of UAE operators already using AI; 79% in the US; 74% in the UK
- Restaurant automation market growing fast, driven by labor shortages and thin margins
- AI agents have crossed from experimental to operational - the technology is ready
- The implementation gap remains - most restaurants need help getting from "interested" to "running"

## 2. The Problem

### 2.1 Staffing is the #1 Crisis

- **62% staff turnover rate** - highest of any sector
- Managers spend 2-4 hours per week on scheduling alone, often getting it wrong
- High turnover means constant onboarding with inconsistent knowledge

### 2.2 Time Wasted on Repetitive Tasks

- **Phone calls:** Missed calls = lost revenue. A busy restaurant may receive 50-100 calls per day. Most go unanswered during service peaks.
- **Inventory management:** Ordering is guesswork. Restaurants over-order to avoid stockouts, tying up cash. 29% of small full-service restaurants are still not using AI for dynamic pricing or forecasting.
- **Reporting:** Area managers spend hours every week pulling data from POS, payroll, and inventory systems.
- **Customer reviews:** Most restaurants never systematically analyze reviews.

### 2.3 Thin Margins Mean Every Inefficiency Costs

- Average restaurant net profit margin: **3-9%**
- Food waste alone can account for 4-10% of total food costs
- Labor typically runs 30-40% of revenue
- 47% of operators say automation is critical to their ability to manage costs (National Restaurant Association, 2024)

### 2.4 Technology Fragmentation

- Restaurants typically use 4-8 different software systems: POS, reservation, inventory, scheduling, delivery aggregators, marketing tools
- None of them talk to each other
- This is exactly the problem AI agents are built to solve

## 3. The Solution

We deploy a suite of AI agents, configured for each restaurant's specific setup, that act as always-on digital staff members handling specific operational tasks.

### 3.1 The Agent Portfolio

**Revenue Protection Agents**

- **Phone Answering Agent** - Handles all incoming calls: reservations, FAQs, takeaway orders. Never misses a call. Routes complex calls to a human. Reduces staff interruptions during service by 60-80%.
- **Voice Order Agent** - For drive-thru and phone orders. Interprets customer intent accurately, sends order directly to POS/KDS. Reduces order errors and increases throughput.
- **Review Response Agent** - Monitors Google, TripAdvisor, OpenTable reviews and drafts personalized, on-brand responses. Flags urgent negative reviews immediately.

**Operations Efficiency Agents**

- **Smart Rota Agent** - Auto-generates optimized weekly schedules based on forecasted demand, employee availability, and labor budget constraints.
- **Inventory Reorder Agent** - Tracks stock usage against sales patterns and shelf life. Recommends just-in-time reorders to reduce waste and free up cash.
- **Prep Volume Forecaster** - Uses historical sales, weather, local events, and booking trends to recommend optimal prep volumes. Targets the 4-10% food waste problem directly.
- **Fridge/Freezer Compliance Agent** - Monitors IoT-connected temperature data. Triggers instant alerts for anomalies. Handles food safety compliance documentation automatically.

**Management & Insight Agents**

- **Weekly P&L Compiler** - Pulls data from POS, scheduling, and inventory systems. Generates executive-ready performance summaries. Saves 2-4 hours of manager time per week per site.
- **Customer Sentiment Monitor** - Scans reviews and survey data, detects trends, alerts managers to specific issues with actionable recommendations.
- **Menu & Pricing Optimizer** - Analyzes item-level margin and sales data to recommend price adjustments and identify underperforming menu items.

**Marketing Agents**

- **Content Generation Agent** - Creates social media posts, email campaigns, and promotional copy in the restaurant's brand voice.
- **Loyalty Engagement Agent** - Identifies regular customers, automates birthday/anniversary outreach, manages loyalty program communications.
- **Dynamic Pricing Agent** - Adjusts special offers, happy hour pricing, and promotional menus based on demand patterns.

### 3.2 How It Actually Works

1. **Discovery (1-2 weeks):** Audit their current tech stack, identify the 3-5 highest-impact agent opportunities
2. **Deployment (1 week):** Agents connected to existing systems (POS, phone, scheduling tools)
3. **Training & Calibration (2-4 weeks):** Agents learn the restaurant's specific voice, procedures, and preferences
4. **Managed Operations:** We monitor agent performance, handle exceptions, and provide monthly reporting

## 6. Go-to-Market Strategy

### 6.1 Beachhead Market

**UK (London initially)** - Henry's base, Unik's network, high density of independent and group restaurants.

**West Africa (Nigeria/Ghana second wave)** - Unik's relationships, rapidly growing hospitality sector, less competition from established US/EU restaurant tech vendors.

### 6.2 How to Reach Restaurants

**Phase 1: Network-Led (Months 1-3)**
- Unik's existing hospitality contacts are the primary channel. Personal introduction > cold outreach.
- Target: Restaurant owners Unik already knows who are complaining about staffing, missed calls, or food costs.

**Phase 2: Proof Points (Months 3-6)**
- Turn the first 3-5 pilot customers into case studies. Before/after data is essential.
- Short video demos (2-3 minutes) showing the agent in action.

**Phase 3: Referral + Inbound (Months 6-12)**
- Formal referral program: Existing customers get 1 month free for each successful referral.
- Partnership with POS vendors as referral partners.

### 6.3 Pilot Strategy

**First 3 pilots - what we're looking for:**
- Medium-complexity: restaurants with 20-100 covers/day, 2-5 staff on at any time, using a POS system
- Owner/manager actively engaged
- A specific pain point they can articulate

**Pilot structure:**
- Week 1: Discovery + system audit
- Week 2: Phone agent deployment (fastest win)
- Weeks 3-4: Additional agents deployed
- Week 8: Review with data
- Month 3: Either expand to full tier or formalize ongoing Core subscription
