# Memory Systems Need Canaries, Not Leaderboards

We operate eight memory integrations: Hermes FTS, OpenViking, QMD, GBrain, Cognee, Honcho, LCM, and LibraVDB. On July 17 the nightly readiness sweep returned 8 of 8 PASS, 58 of 58 cases passed.

Two days earlier, OpenViking and QMD were blocked. The all-green sweep exists because four providers were repaired and the full sweep was rerun.

## The status taxonomy

**PASS.** Adapter started, health responded, known fact written and retrieved, cleanup succeeded.

**BLOCKED.** Precondition absent. Service down, endpoint unreachable. Not an adapter quality signal.

**DEGRADED.** Part of the path works. Health responds but retrieval is stale. Write succeeds but read times out.

**FAIL.** Contract violated. Provider was reachable and returned wrong results.

## Delta-based alerting

Alert on status transitions, not every green run. OpenViking's sequence: BLOCKED, PASS, BLOCKED, PASS. QMD's: BLOCKED, DEGRADED, PASS, BLOCKED, PASS. Those sequences are the operational signal.
