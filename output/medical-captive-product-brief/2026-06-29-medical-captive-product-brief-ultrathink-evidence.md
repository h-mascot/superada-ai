## Context and company situation
- Andrew says Medical Captive is in the second of seven straight weeks of renewal-season chaos.
- Renewal season begins with gathering information through May, running data through May, creating initial indications, then firming up with data through July.
- Clients often dislike July data and ask Medical Captive to look at August, September, and October too.
- Andrew says their fourth quarter effectively starts in mid-April and ends around December 12 to 15.

- Medical Captive is small but rapidly growing: took 10 years to get to roughly $15M, then one year to $25M, and expects to approach $40M next year.
- Biggest concern: scalability.

## Current workflow and pain
- They do not take inbound calls or inbound submissions. Submissions arrive through email and are handled by a back office.
- Their workflow includes email, DocuSign, spreadsheets, proposals, trading with partners, and broker/client communications.
- Andrew used to create a key spreadsheet twice a week. Now he creates it about 35 times a week.
- Spreadsheet size grew from about 200 lines to close to 2,500.
- The spreadsheet is a legacy marker and not the most effective way forward.
- Headcount moved from roughly 1.3 FTE to 6 FTE and may double again over the next 2 to 3 years.
- Main operational need: repeat accurately, preserve historical consistency, reduce manual proposal generation and distribution load.
- They receive packets of information and check them against must-haves, want-haves, and good-to-haves. Another provider is already working on the intake/checklist scoring side.
- Andrew is more interested in back-office generation and distribution than intake.
- Many renewals produce 3, 4, or 5 separate proposals per entity.
- If 30 entities each have 5 proposals, that is 150 outputs.
- If each output takes 1.5 hours to review, generate, proofread, authorize, and send, August and September run out of hours.
- Different team members create different outputs from the same sheet because of individual bias and skill differences.
- Handing renewal generation from individual A to individual B creates muddiness during variance handling.

## Security and data concerns
- Andrew is concerned about privileged/protected data.
- He asked whether Curacel can bulk erase their data 45 days after project completion.
- He also asked whether Curacel trains models on customer data.
- Henry answered that Curacel does not need the data for model training and can delete data when the workflow does not require long-term retention.
- Henry stated Curacel has HIPAA, SOC 2, and ISO compliance.

## Desired future state
- A secure broker portal where brokers can review proposal options.
- Broker can choose the option they want, push a button, and generate a final PDF.
- The PDF is delivered to the broker email box and to the client automatically.
- Medical Captive keeps a legacy copy on its system.
- Once the final option is selected, the other options should go away to prevent cross-contamination, wrong dollar amounts, and wrong columns across revisions.
- Blue-sky concept: initial 1.0 source-of-truth renewal, then each additional iteration/modification becomes a dropdown menu on a website.
- When broker and client feel confident, they generate the final package.
