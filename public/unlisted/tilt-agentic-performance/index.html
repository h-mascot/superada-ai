What we know

Evidence from Liz’s first meeting notes and Tilt’s own agenda. This is not a cold pitch anymore; they are inviting a scoping/design session.

Evidence

They want AI-native operating change

Founder/CTO sponsorship is strong. The competitive fear is “2–3 people shops building AI-native from day 1,” not incumbents.

Evidence

They have stack gravity

They are fully on Claude + Cowork, moving away from OpenAI, not using Gemini, and have a CFO-driven context repo.

Evidence

Adoption is uneven

Leadership adoption is strong, middle layer weaker. Training is clunky. Engineers fear losing craft.

Evidence

The process pain is named

“Everyone hates performance reviews and feedback.” They care about promotion transparency and time saved.

Evidence

The agenda is explicit

Scope/design an outcome-based agentic workflow for transforming Performance Management through AI.

Inference

The real gap is structure

They do not need another AI pep talk. They need an operating workflow that turns “shooting from the hip” into trusted change.

Objective & asks

What Tilt is explicitly asking, plus the implied job behind it.

Inferred objective

Use Performance Management as a high-leverage proof point for AI-native work: clearer performance signals, lower admin burden, better promotion transparency, and a workflow managers trust.

Their asks

  • Explicit: define problem space, scope opportunity, identify MVP use case, define success criteria.
  • Implicit: operationalize “performance signals.”
  • Implicit: reduce manager + employee admin burden.
  • Implicit: fit Claude/Cowork/context repo.
  • Implicit: avoid surveillance/craft-replacement blowback.

Recommended MVP

Make managers more evidenced and consistent. Do not make AI the judge.

Recommended

Performance Signal & Review Calibration Agent

An agentic workflow that assembles source-backed evidence, maps it to the rubric, flags weak claims/missing signals, reduces manager prep time, and creates calibration packets for People.

  • Manager pre-review brief
  • Evidence map against rubric
  • Missing-signal checklist
  • Unsupported-claim flags
  • Coaching prompts for feedback delivery
  • Calibration packet + audit trail
Do not do

AI performance decisioning

Phase one should explicitly exclude high-risk decision automation.

  • No automated ratings
  • No promotion/comp/PIP/termination recommendations
  • No opaque employee scoring
  • No raw Claude-usage-as-performance metric
  • No private DM surveillance

If they accept: next few weeks

A bounded path from meeting commitment to a scale/no-scale decision.

24–48h

Diagnostic map

Interview Liz/Gena/Ben/Sam + managers. Map review workflow, signals, data, pain, and red lines.

Week 2

MVP design

Select cohort and shadow/live mode. Design templates, baseline, evaluation rubric, governance.

Configure workflow around existing stack. Run on historical/sample/opt-in pilot data with audit trails.

Readout

Assess time saved, evidence quality, trust, failure modes, tech fit. Expand, adjust, or stop.

Use this language

The words matter because people-data + AI can get creepy fast.

Opener

“Your agenda already points to Performance Management as the high-leverage area. Our goal today should be to leave with a concrete MVP workflow, not a generic AI transformation plan. Since the agenda is explicitly about performance signals and admin burden, I’d suggest we test review prep and calibration as the first wedge — manager judgment with better evidence, not AI deciding performance.”

Close

“If we agree this is the right wedge, the next step should be a one-page pilot charter: MVP workflow, cohort, data in/out, outputs, success metrics, controls, timeline, and the approval path. Then you can decide quickly whether this is worth funding as a bounded pilot.”

Risks, blind spots, falsifiers

Things to surface early so the room trusts the process.

CategoryWhat to watchMeeting question
DataUnknown HRIS/performance tool, rubric maturity, Cowork/context repo shape.“Where does review evidence live today, and what is approved for AI use?”
TrustAI scoring, surveillance, raw Claude-usage performance metrics, engineer craft fears.“What should be explicitly off-limits in phase one?”
TimingUnknown review-cycle calendar and urgency.“What timeline matters for the next performance cycle?”
Buyer pathBudget owner unclear: People, AI Enablement, Mac/CFO, founders.“If the charter is compelling, who needs to approve the pilot?”
